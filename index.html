01 — What is it

What is the Buildathon
at ShopX 2026?

Right after the morning keynotes end, ShopX turns into a build floor. Six teams get one afternoon to design, build, and demo a real AI workflow for ecommerce — then pitch it live to a panel of operators and investors.

Real workflows
Not a slide deck
Every team builds on production AI tools and live Shopify APIs — the output is a working product, not a mockup.
Mixed-expertise teams
Sellers + builders, paired
Commerce operators who live the problem team up with app partners who can ship the fix — in the same afternoon.
Live judging
3 minutes, no slides
Each team demos live to a judging panel at the Demo Expo. Scored on the spot, awards announced the same day.
02 — Team format

6 teams. 4 builders each.

Every team pairs the people who feel the problem with the people who can build the fix — so the workflow gets shaped by a real seller pain point from hour one.

How a team comes together

Each of the 6 teams is built from attendee sellers and partner-side builders — matched ahead of the sprint so everyone walks in knowing their role.

The 2 app partner members lead the build — they set technical direction and keep the sprint moving toward a working demo by 4 PM.

Team matching happens ahead of the event based on track interest and company fit.
