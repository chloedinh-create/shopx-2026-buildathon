FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is the ShopX 2026 AI Buildathon?+

The ShopX 2026 AI Buildathon is a hands-on afternoon where Shopify sellers work with expert mentors. Together, they turn real store use cases into practical AI workflows or mini-apps that sellers can adapt and test in their own stores.

Is this a competition?+

No. The focus is learning by doing, building around a real use case, and taking a practical starting point back to your store. Teams share their builds and learn from one another.

Who is the Buildathon for?+

The Buildathon is for Shopify sellers and ecommerce operators who want practical guidance on applying AI to a real store challenge. It is designed for people who understand their store needs but may not know which AI workflow or tool to use.

Do I need coding experience?+

No. Coding experience is not required. Mentors guide you through the tools, workflow, and build process.

What will I build?+

You will build a practical AI workflow or mini-app based on a real use case from your store. The specific output depends on your track, use case, and available tools.

Who are the mentors?+

Mentors are experts from leading AI, ecommerce, and Shopify apps. They help teams choose the right approach, use the tools, and turn each use case into a practical build.

How are teams matched?+

Sellers are matched before the event based on their store context, the use case they want to improve, and the track that best fits their needs. Each team works with mentors who can guide that use case.

What should I bring?+

Bring a real store challenge or use case you want to improve. You do not need a finished idea or technical solution.

What happens after the Buildathon?+

You leave with a practical workflow or mini-app starting point, clear next steps, and a certificate of participation. You can continue adapting and testing the build in your store after the event.

CERTIFICATE

Leave With Something You Can Use.

Every seller and mentor receives an official ShopX 2026 Buildathon Certificate of Participation. More importantly, you will leave with a practical AI workflow or mini-app to adapt and test in your store.
