WHAT IS IT

What is the AI Buildathon
at ShopX 2026?

The morning is keynotes and panels. Right after lunch, ShopX turns into a build floor — six mixed teams take a real store problem from idea to a working AI workflow, then present it to the judges.

Real Workflows
> Connecting to Shopify API...✓ Connected
> Analyzing product data with AI...✓ Connected
> Generating content & campaigns...✓ Connected
> Deploying workflow...✓ Connected

Not a Slide Deck

Every team builds on production AI tools and live Shopify APIs. What you show at the end is a working product, not a mockup.

Mixed Teams

Sellers + Builders, Paired

Commerce operators who live the problem team up with mentors who can ship the fix same afternoon, same table.

Live Judging

Present & Q&A

From 15:10 each team presents its build and takes questions from the judges. Awards are announced at 16:30, same day.

SCHEDULE

The Afternoon, Hour By Hour.

The Buildathon runs across the full afternoon block of ShopX 2026.

Buildathon Kickoff
